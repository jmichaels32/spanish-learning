How the score is calculated

Frequency: conversational subtitle frequency plus the ESCOW web corpus. Learner exposure: early-weighted A1–C1 appearances in ELELex textbooks and simplified readers.

Conjugation value: measured differences from a regular paradigm. Drill fit: explicit penalties for auxiliaries, impersonal verbs, and verbs normally used in special constructions. Distinctiveness: a small penalty for manually documented near-synonyms.

Candidate scope is based on subtitle frequency. Learner evidence comes from ELELex; web frequency comes from TAALES Español / ESCOW. Missing learner evidence scores zero. The complete generated dataset and formula are inspectable in the repository.
